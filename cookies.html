This Cookies Policy explains how OneMajlis uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device that help websites function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for core site functionality, such as remembering your session while you browse.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you've made, such as language or display settings.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it over time.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the OneMajlis website.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services we use, such as analytics providers. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies governing their use of your information.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
